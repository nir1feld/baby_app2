# BabyLog

BabyLog is a small open-source Android app for parents to log a baby's feeds,
diapers and sleep.

- Logging is instant and works offline, because the app keeps its log on the
  phone.
- Parents on different phones, each with their own Google account, share one
  log through a Google Sheet they own.
- The sheet has readable Feeds, Diapers and Sleep tabs for charts.

The app is shared directly as an APK file, not through an app store.
